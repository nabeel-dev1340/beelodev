Invoice automation starts with a clear definition of what your accounting team needs. Capturing text from a document is one step; validating it and moving it into the right process are separate responsibilities.

A useful pilot should show the whole path using representative invoices and agreed approval rules.

## Define the intake

List where invoices arrive: email, a shared folder, a vendor portal, or a manual upload. Agree on how the system identifies a new invoice and what it does with attachments that are not invoices.

If the main task is downloading invoices from a portal, [Bulk Document Downloads](/services/bulk-document-downloads) may be enough. Extraction and posting to accounting software belong in an additional scope.

## Choose the required fields

Specify the fields the destination needs. These may include supplier identity, invoice number, currency, dates, line items, tax, and totals.

Review invoices from different suppliers during the pilot. Include credit notes, unusual layouts, missing fields, and multi-page documents where those appear in your actual workload.

## Validate before posting

Define the checks your team performs today. A workflow might compare totals, match purchase orders, or look for duplicate invoice numbers within a supplier account.

Uncertain extraction should enter an exception path. The goal is a clear, reviewable result; automatically posting every output is not always appropriate.

## Preserve approvals

Identify the conditions that need human sign-off, such as amount thresholds, a new supplier, or a purchase order mismatch. Document who approves and where the decision is recorded.

Check the destination software and available integration methods before promising an import. The pilot should prove that a sample invoice reaches the correct place with its supporting document and audit context.

## Estimate the actual effort

Measure current handling time and include time spent resolving exceptions. The [invoice processing cost calculator](/invoice-processing-cost-calculator) can estimate manual labor cost from your inputs.

Any savings assessment also needs implementation, review, integration, and ongoing maintenance costs. Avoid treating the full manual labor estimate as guaranteed savings.

## Hand over the exception process

The operating instructions should show how to inspect a failed invoice, correct its data, and retry without posting a duplicate. Agree on how vendor format changes and destination updates are maintained.
